# Regions: what flies, what it costs, when to go

> Researched 2026-11-02. Award charts and surcharges change constantly. Re-verify before any transfer. Every figure is dated or flagged.

---

## JAPAN

**The only region where real First class is both available and reachable from his points.**

### Products
ANA First "The Suite" flies SFO to both HND and NRT, but equipment alternates and only about 8 to 10 of ANA's 13 777-300ERs carry it, so the specific flight must be checked. JAL First from SFO exists but is fragile: it rides the 777-300ER, which is being retired, and the newer A350-1000 First suite serves JFK, DFW, LAX, CDG and LHR but **not** SFO.

### Ways in, cheapest real cost first

| Path | Currency | Miles one-way | Cash | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Atmos → JAL First** | **Bilt** | **~110,000** | **$20** | No surcharge pass-through. Best real cost. The 110,000 is arithmetic from a verified published distance band (5,001-7,000 mi, SFO-NRT ≈ 5,130), **not a published city-pair price** |
| **Virgin Atlantic → ANA First** | Both | **72,500** | **unconfirmed** | 145,000 round trip, halved for one-way. **Phone booking only.** Whether Virgin passes ANA's $419 surcharge is the single unknown that decides if this is the best deal or a trap |
| ANA Mileage Club | **Amex only** | 85,000 Regular | **+$419/flight** | ~$800+ round trip in cash |
| Atmos → JAL Business | Bilt | 75,000 | $20 | Same band, business |
| ANA Business | Amex only | 50,000-52,500 | +$419/flight | |

ANA's official chart, read off ana.co.jp 2026-11-02, Japan ↔ North America round trip: Business 100,000 Low / 105,000 Regular / 165,000 High. First 150,000 / 170,000 / **300,000**. One-way is half. **The round-trip-only requirement died 24 June 2025**, so one-ways are now allowed at half price. Round-the-world awards were killed in the same change.

### Season cliff
See SKILL.md section 4. **Dec 18 is Regular at 85,000 First one-way. Dec 19 is High at 150,000.** Jan 6 onward drops to Low at 75,000.

### Availability
ANA releases roughly one First seat per flight at 355 days. Inside a few months, everything is cancellation space. Two seats together is rare at any time.

### When to go

Tokyo December: ~58mm rain, highs near 12°C, one of the driest and sunniest months. January: ~48mm, driest of the year, highs ~10°C, lows 2-3°C. Kyoto runs 3-5°C colder in all months.

**2026 is unusual.** Japan Meteorological Corporation forecast, issued 2 Sept 2026, puts autumn foliage running late:

| City | Maple peak | Ginkgo peak |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo | 28-29 Nov | 26 Nov |
| **Kyoto** | **11 Dec** | 27 Nov |
| Osaka | 4 Dec | 24 Nov |
| Nagano | 21 Nov (9 days late) | 14 Nov |
| Sapporo | 7 Nov | 6 Nov |

Normally Kyoto peaks mid-to-late November, which is also the crowd peak. **In 2026 the crowd peak and colour peak have separated by about two weeks.** International crowds drop sharply after the first week of December. That gap is the opportunity, and it exists only this year.

**Risk:** all three sources carry the same early-September forecast. As of 2 Nov nobody had published an observed-colour report confirming it is verifying. Check n-kishou.com before booking.

Inbound tailwind: Japan arrivals Jan-Sep 2026 were 30.7 million, **down 2.9% year on year**, with Chinese arrivals down 55.5%. Late 2026 is materially less crowded than late 2025.

### Avoid: 28 Dec - 4 Jan
Not weather, infrastructure. **JR Central runs Nozomi reserved-seat-only 25 Dec 2026 to 5 Jan 2027**, killing walk-on Shinkansen travel. Government offices close 29 Dec - 3 Jan. Toyosu market closed 31 Dec and 1-4 Jan. Hotels 20-40% above baseline. Return travel peaks 2-4 Jan with **3 January the worst single day**. Prices reset around 4-5 January.

**Best window: 5-13 December.** Maples at peak in Kyoto, crowds gone, Regular award season, well clear of the 19 Dec cliff.

## AUSTRALIA

**Real First exists, but not from SFO.** Qantas First is A380-only. SFO-SYD is a 787-9, 3x weekly, no First cabin. MEL-LAX A380 ended 24 Oct 2026. That leaves SYD-LAX and SYD-DFW, so a positioning flight is mandatory.

| Path | Currency | Miles | Cash | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Atmos → Qantas First LAX-SYD** | **Bilt** | **130,000** | **~$48** | **The standout value in the whole study.** Corroborated by a June 2026 award alert pricing it at exactly this |
| Atmos → Qantas Business | Bilt | 85,000 | ~$48 | 7,001-10,000 mi band |
| Qantas Frequent Flyer | **Amex only** | 130,100 business LAX-SYD | + Classic Plus carrier charges | First rate **not confirmed**; AFF says First runs ~50% above business, so ~195,000 is an estimate, not a rate |

American AAdvantage prices Qantas First at 110,000 plus ~$34, but AAdvantage reaches neither Amex nor Bilt. Out of range.

**Unverified lead worth a manual look:** LoyaltyLobby reported 26 Oct 2026 that Flying Blue can now book Qantas First. Flying Blue takes both currencies, so this would matter. The page blocks fetches.

### Availability
Qantas releases long-haul Business and First in **batches close to departure**, to higher status tiers first, no-status last. Roughly 14 First suites on one daily flight. Atmos users report Qantas awards sometimes not showing.

### When to go
Sydney December: 26.0/18.0°C, 74mm. Melbourne: 24.2°C, 51mm. Both drier than November.

**Window: 1-17 December. Hard stop before the 18th.** School terms end 11 Dec in Queensland, 17-18 Dec in NSW and Victoria. **22 Dec - 26 Jan is flat-out peak**, with rates reported 30-150% above off-peak.

**Avoid the tropical north entirely in this window.** Cairns January: **379mm of rain**, 31.6°C, 23.7°C overnight. December 178mm. Cyclone season runs 1 Nov to 30 April, plus marine stingers and park closures. The north's good season is May-October.

## SOUTH AMERICA

**No real First class.** Business is the top cabin: United Polaris, Copa "Dreams" lie-flat 737 MAX 9, LATAM 787-9, AA Flagship Business.

The one piece of genuine First anywhere in the region is Swiss First on the fifth-freedom GRU-EZE hop, bookable with United miles. A fun footnote, not a transpacific-grade product.

**Easiest region to actually get a premium award.** United hubs at SFO and runs SFO-IAH-EZE/SCL in Polaris. Copa flies lie-flat from SFO and LAX to Panama City with onward connections to GRU, EZE, SCL and Bogota.

| Program | Currency | Cost | Surcharge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aeroplan | Both | 40,000-60,000 one-way by distance | **None on partners** |
| Avianca LifeMiles | Both | varies | **None** |
| Flying Blue | Both | 40,000s northern, 70,000s southern | low |
| United | **Bilt only** | varies | **None** |

Atmos is no longer useful here. LATAM left the Atmos partner list, reportedly September 2025.

### When to go
November is the shoulder almost everywhere: Rio 28/22°C and 100mm before the domestic holidays, El Calafate 16/4°C and 5mm, Cusco at the tail of the dry season.

December ramps into peak, January is full peak with Brazilian school holidays, packed beaches, highest heat and rain.

**Carnival 2027 is 7-9 February**, so it does not touch a trip ending in early January. What does hit is **Reveillon**: 1 January 2027 falls on a Friday, creating a three-day weekend that is among Rio's busiest hotel stretches, with a recommended booking lead time of six to nine months.

**Avoid:** Rio roughly 26 Dec - 5 Jan. Cusco or the Inca Trail in January, 160mm and the wet-season maximum with landslide risk.

## EASTERN EUROPE

**No real First class.** No carrier flies a First cabin to Warsaw, Prague, Budapest or Bucharest. Business via a connection is the ceiling.

**The Lufthansa workaround is dead.** Since 1 June 2026 Lufthansa keeps First award space for Miles & More members only, and no credit card program transfers to Miles & More. Partner business saver space on Lufthansa, Austrian and SWISS was also heavily restricted in July 2026, damaging the Star Alliance path generally.

| Program | Currency | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turkish Miles&Smiles | **Bilt only** | ~65,000 one-way to IST | **+$200-250 surcharge**, then onward |
| LOT via LifeMiles/Aeroplan/ANA | Both | from ~50,000 each way | Reportedly no partner fuel surcharges |
| Iberia Avios | Both | 40,500 off-peak to Madrid | Then connect |
| Flying Blue | Both | 60,000 standard, occasional 45,000 Promo | |

All secondary-source. Check live.

### When to go
The real cost here is light, not rain. Precipitation is low, 35-45mm a month, less than Sydney or Tokyo. But **Krakow gets one hour of sunshine a day in December and eight hours of daylight.** Budapest on 21 December: sunrise 7:28, sunset 15:55.

**Christmas market dates, 2026/27 — the four cities are not aligned, and this is an expensive trap:**

| City | Opens | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| Vienna Rathausplatz | 13 Nov 2026 | **26 Dec 2026** |
| Budapest Vörösmarty tér | 13 Nov 2026 | 31 Dec 2026 |
| Budapest Advent Bazilika | 13 Nov 2026 | 1 Jan 2027 |
| **Prague** | 28 Nov 2026 | **6 Jan 2027** |
| **Krakow** | 27 Nov 2026 (provisional) | **6 Jan 2027** (provisional) |

**An early-January Central Europe trip has to be Prague or Krakow.** Arriving in Vienna on 29 December expecting a Christmas market means arriving to an ice rink.

Most Vienna markets are only fully operating from 20 November, despite the 13 Nov opening.

**Window: 28 Nov - 12 December, Prague or Krakow.** Markets fully running, before the 15-23 Dec crush and the Christmas closures. Weekdays.

Official city tourism sites were all unreachable when researched. Re-check trhypraha.cz and christkindlmarkt.at before booking.

## HONG KONG — worth considering as an Asia alternative

Cathay First flies SFO-HKG on the 777-300ER, six seats. **Atmos publishes it at 70,000 points one-way plus roughly $80-120**, which on paper is the best First price anywhere in this document. Bilt-only. Sources range 70,000 to 130,000 and one says phone booking, so verify.

**The catch is inventory, not price.** A six-month availability study reported Cathay First space as essentially nonexistent, and a live point.me search for 5 December 2026 returned no Cathay at all, in any cabin. Keep the 70,000 figure as a standing alert target, not a plan.

### When to go
October to early December is the consensus best window. December has the lowest humidity of the year at about 67%; January is the driest month at roughly 23mm and runs 15-20°C. The trade-off is winter haze, and skies can be grey when pollution sits under the winter pattern. **Christmas week through 1 January holds peak prices and crowds; early-to-mid January is quiet and cheap** before Lunar New Year ramps up.

## Difficulty ranking, Nov 2026 - early Jan 2027

Easiest to hardest for actually securing a premium award:

1. **South America.** Most carriers, lowest mileage, several zero-surcharge programs, United hub and Copa lie-flat both direct from SFO. No single-aircraft chokepoint.
2. **Japan.** Business genuinely attainable at 50,000-52,500 one-way with many daily SFO flights. First is one seat per flight with the advance window long closed, so it depends on cancellation space.
3. **Eastern Europe.** Mechanically doable in business, but every leg connects, Lufthansa Group has choked partner inventory, Turkish adds real cash, and this is peak Christmas transatlantic.
4. **Australia.** One aircraft type, effectively one First route, ~14 suites daily, batch releases close to departure, status served first, positioning flight required, and Bilt only reaches Qantas through Atmos.

**If First is mandatory, this collapses:** Japan is the only realistic shot, Australia is a long shot needing both a positioning flight and luck, and South America and Eastern Europe are impossible on these currencies.

## Live availability check, 3 November 2026

Run on point.me against real balances. **Every chart price above failed to appear.** See SKILL.md section 4A for the full table. Summary:

- **SFO-Tokyo, 5 Dec:** no ANA First, no JAL First, at any price. Best 150,000 + $11 on a 28h35m 1-stop.
- **SFO-Tokyo, 10 Jan:** same 150,000, but 15h25m. **January buys a materially better trip for identical points.**
- **SFO-Hong Kong, 5 Dec:** no Cathay in any cabin. Best 97,000 on a 2-stop part-economy itinerary.
- **LAX-Sydney, 5 Dec:** no Qantas in any cabin. Best 330,000 Fiji business. Australia First is off the table for this winter.
- **SFO-Rio, 28 Dec:** best 207,000 AA business, 2 stops, 25h44m. Confirms New Year's as the worst-value window of the year.

### Qantas First, the specific reason it never appears
Award seats release 353 days out, and **premium-cabin release goes to Gold, Platinum and Platinum One first**. Members with no status typically do not see the initial release at all. Later releases happen a few weeks out but are unreliable. Melbourne's last First-equipped A380 departure was 24 October 2026, leaving SYD-LAX and SYD-DFW. For a no-status solo flyer six weeks out, Qantas First is not a plan.

### New Year's in South America, the honest read
Rio Réveillon is the single hardest ask in this document. Copacabana-facing rooms sell **four to twelve months ahead**, hotels sell 26 Dec - 3 Jan as minimum-stay packages, and prices double to triple. 1 January 2027 falls on a Friday, making it a three-day weekend. There is no First cabin to South America at all, and live business pricing for 28 December ran 207,000-313,000 one-way.

**November is the better South America window by every measure:** shoulder season, 50,000-65,000 one-way business via Aeroplan or LifeMiles with no fuel surcharges, both reachable from either currency.

If New Year's itself is the point rather than Rio specifically, **Valparaíso** is the alternative worth pricing: roughly 15 launch points across Valparaíso and Viña del Mar, described variously as the second-largest display on the continent or the largest in Latin America, and nothing like as hard to book. Buenos Aires (rooftops, tango clubs) and Cartagena (midnight effigy burning) are the other two that come up consistently.

## The Philippines note

Rainy season is **June to October**. November through January is the dry half, and the usual instinct that Nov-Jan is rainy there is inverted.

The real exception: the **east coast** gets its rainfall maximum December to February. Siargao is Climate Type II with no pronounced dry season, reportedly 650-800mm a month in Dec-Jan. Eastern Samar behaves the same. December typhoons have hit the Visayas more than once, including Phanfone on Christmas Eve 2019.

So "Philippines in rainy season" points at June-October, but "Siargao in December" is a correct thing to avoid.
