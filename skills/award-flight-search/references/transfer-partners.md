### Excise fee
$0.0006 per point, **capped at $99**, applies only to **Delta and JetBlue**. Cap bites at 165,000 points.

### Transfer speed — sources genuinely disagree

| Partner | Tracker A (15 Oct 2026) | Tracker B (27 Jul 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| ANA | 1-2 days | Instant |
| Singapore | Instant | 15 days |
| Aer Lingus | Instant | 4 days |

Both agree Aeroplan, Avianca, BA, Delta, JetBlue, Qatar and Virgin Atlantic are effectively instant, and Aeromexico is slow. **Assume a delay is possible on ANA and Singapore.** Award space can vanish during a multi-day transfer, which is exactly the scenario that strands points.

## Bilt Rewards — 19 airline partners, all 1:1, 2,000 minimum

Aer Lingus, Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France/KLM Flying Blue, **Atmos Rewards (Alaska + Hawaiian)**, Avianca LifeMiles, British Airways, **Cathay Pacific**, **Emirates**, Etihad Guest, Iberia, **Japan Airlines Mileage Bank**, **JetBlue**, Qatar Airways, Southwest (up to 72 hrs), TAP Miles&Go, Turkish Miles&Smiles, United MileagePlus, Virgin Atlantic.

Virgin Red appears on one tracker but not another. It is a Virgin Group program rather than an airline program. **UNCERTAIN.**

Hotels: Marriott 1:1, Hilton 1:1, IHG 1:1, Wyndham 1:1, Accor 3:2, I Prefer 1:2, Amtrak 2:1.

**World of Hyatt drops from 1:1 to 4:3 on 1 January 2027.** No Palladium exception. Existing balances untouched, only post-1-Jan conversions reprice. Move points before 31 December 2026 if there is a Hyatt use case.

### Gone from Bilt
- **American AAdvantage ended 24 June 2024.** Any source still listing AA as a Bilt partner is years stale.
- **Spirit is gone entirely.** Ceased operations 2 May 2026, Chapter 7. Bilt had added them in December 2025; it lasted five months.

Alaska rebranded Mileage Plan to **Atmos Rewards** on 20 Aug 2025, now covering Hawaiian. Bilt is one of very few transferable currencies feeding it. Amex, Chase and Citi do not.

## The overlap: 11 programs both currencies reach

Virgin Atlantic, Aeroplan, Flying Blue, Avianca LifeMiles, British Airways, Iberia, Qatar, Aer Lingus, Cathay, Emirates, JetBlue.

**Pooling targets, ranked:**
1. **Air Canada Aeroplan.** Both 1:1, both instant, broadest partner access, no fuel surcharges on many partners. Best all-round.
2. **The Avios family** (BA, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Qatar). Four overlaps feeding one combinable pool.
3. **Virgin Atlantic.** Both 1:1, and the cheapest published ANA First rate.
4. **Flying Blue and LifeMiles.** Both 1:1 from both.

**Never substitute:** Cathay, Emirates and JetBlue go from **Bilt**. Amex clips 20%.
