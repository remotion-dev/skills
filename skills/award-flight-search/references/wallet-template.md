## Preferences

```

Willing to position to LAX?           (required for Qantas First)
```

The positioning question matters more than it looks. Qantas First does not fly from SFO at all, so the answer decides whether Australia is on the table.

## Season preference

Stated preference: **the edge of a season.** Not peak, not the dead tail of it. Explicitly fine with off-season as long as it is not the worst of it.

Worth recording any hard blackout dates, since award charts have season cliffs that can be worth tens of thousands of miles for a one-day shift.
